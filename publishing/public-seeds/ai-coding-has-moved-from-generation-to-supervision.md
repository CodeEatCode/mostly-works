## Core claim

The practical AI engineering shift for 2026 is not that tools can generate more
code; it is that engineers need better supervision loops around agentic work.

## Allowed material

- Public 2026 trend framing from Capgemini and Thoughtworks Technology Radar Vol. 34
- Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025 adoption, trust, and frustration signals
- DORA 2025 AI-assisted software development framing around AI as an amplifier
- Martin Fowler fragments on humans working on the loop, harness engineering, and internal quality
- OWASP LLM application risks around prompt injection, excessive agency, and overreliance
- Existing public Code Eat Code themes on TDD, CI rules, architecture drift, and AI delivery blockers
- Generic supervision practices: scoped tasks, tests, CI gates, least privilege, review boundaries, and audit trails

## Avoid

- Employer, client, customer, or project examples
- Internal automation logs or source-specific retrieval details
- Private repository names, local paths, calendar details, or personal identifiers
- Unverified claims about model capability, market share, or vendor roadmaps
- Security advice that implies agents can be made safe with prompts alone
